# The Crumbling Moon

> *A very worn book. The majority depicts poorly illustrated instructions on how to perform several different rituals. One page even shows the steps for the petrification ritual.* 
>*The only written words are in [[Languages|Common]] and it merely describes the chants to say.*
>*However, the very first page has the following.* 

---

## The Garden is new
As familiar as it is new to us. We will learn to call it home.

## The Garden is unwanted
But a weed in the eye of the cosmos. Something neither desirable or worthy of attention.

## The Garden is delicate
Fragile as a new born. The trees are yet to reach the sky.

## The Garden is crumbling
Its roots will never set, for the soil will forever be disturbed.

## The Garden is protected
We are its defenders. We will sure the foundation.

**We will save the Crumbling Moon**
