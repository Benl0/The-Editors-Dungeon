# The Crumbling Moon

## The Garden is new
As familiar as it is new to us. We will learn to call it home.

## The Garden is unwanted
But a weed in the eye of the cosmos. Something neither desirable or worthy of attention.

## The Garden is delicate
Fragile as a newborn. The trees are yet to reach the sky.

## The Garden is crumbling
Its roots will never set, for the soil will forever be disturbed.

## The Garden is protected
We are its defenders. We will sure the foundation.

**We will save the Crumbling Moon**
