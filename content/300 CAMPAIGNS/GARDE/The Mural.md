# Glyphs as old as the World


At the back of [[Cathedral.jpg|The Cathedral]], beyond a broken magic circle, sits several stone tablets. Engraved and chiselled onto them, a scene of epic proportions.

> An unfathomable evil sweeping across the lands. Countless dead and many more ensnared.
> A rebellion formed, lead by eight great heroes.
> However, its power proved too dreadful and seemingly all the heroes perished in the fight.

There are no more murals.
Nothing like this is recorded anywhere is [[Garde|Garde's]] history.
