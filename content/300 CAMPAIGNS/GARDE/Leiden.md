# Island of Leiden
Often the centre of many wars and conflicts. Besides a handful of settlements, Leiden features one oppressive fortress. A key military outpost that has long been overrun by [[Orc]] bandits and slavers.
Many kingdoms have tried to reclaim the island but all assaults and sieges proved too costly. Both in coin and lives.
