# Minutes

- Eliza was lost in thought, looking at [[The Mural]].
- [[Sylvaris Nightwhisper|Sylvaris]] was able to salvage all the robes from the [[The Crumbling Moon|cultists]]. Six in total.
- [[Nyx Timber Ku|Ku]] found the [[Crumbling Moon Manifesto]], detailing some of their motivations.
- Ku, [[Eliza Trotz|Eliza]] and [[Winslow]] were able to discover faint life energy coming from the statues.
- The Party discovered a map, highlighting four points of interests in the far opposite corners of the world. 
	- [[Leiden]], [[Morndoom Peak]], [[The Scarred Archipelago]] and [[The Frozen Wastes]].
- Eliza and Sylvaris opened up to each other, finally letting some honesty through their hard shells.
- [[Dane Sparkfire|Dane]] finds the necklace he made for his sister, [[Arla]]. She was here.
- The party loads the one intact statue on the back of a horse drawn carriage and brings it back to [[Haven]].
- Ku learned the dangers of raw magic water and how it can cause a wild magic effect.
- With the help of [[David Starfate]], the party was able to deduce that the statues are real people, or at least were.
	- Using [[Dispel Magic]] and a small piece revealed its true, original form, before dissolving to dust.
- The [[Broken Wand]] the party found most likely held the exact petrification magic used on the statue.
	- However, it's strange. Similar to [[Plane Shift]], but in reverse. As if it's trying to keep the target in the same plane of existence.
- The Party comes to a decision. To find out more, they will follow the cult's map to [[Leiden]].
